project: PATHWAY PLANNER
Anyone can use the learning pathway planner mobile app for many different learning platforms, but it excels in school and college formats. 

Once you have an idea of what you want to accomplish and where, enter the time period or "term" you are tracking your courses/seminars, and add the requirements as a "course" to that "term". Start at any point. From high school to your next college, community college to university, or bachelor's to PHD.

Download and install on an Android device. Then, begin entering your terms and courses. Track your progress, create and share notes, search for courses by name to find them quickly if you have many, and see the report of classes you need to complete and the classes you have already completed.

1. You will need an Android device with Android 9 or above and an internet connection.
2. On your Android, Go to your device's Settings > Security > More Security Settings > Install Unknown Apps > Check/enable the app you want to open the .APK from to allow installations from sources other than the Google Play Store. If opening from the browser you will need to enable the browser. NOTE: After installing, it is recommended to turn this feature off. 
3. Once downloaded, find the APK file in your device’s Downloads folder, Install the APK: Tap on the APK file to begin the installation process, and follow the on-screen instructions. 
4. Create Terms and Courses: Open the app and start creating terms for your learning. 
5. Add courses to each term as needed.
6. Update as update your progress within each course.
7. Search for courses by name to find them quickly.
8. View your progress and completed courses in the report section.
